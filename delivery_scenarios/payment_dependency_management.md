# Payment Dependency Management

## Scenario
Managing external payment provider dependencies during sprint execution.

## Context
E-commerce payment platform integrating Gift Card, Credit Card, PayPal, and Apple Pay using Java Spring Boot microservices. Multiple Scrum teams with external vendor dependencies.

## Challenge
Sprint commitments were frequently at risk due to:
- Delays in external payment gateway certification
- Incomplete API readiness from vendors
- Tight security and compliance timelines

These dependencies caused blocked stories and spillover across sprints.

## Action Taken
- Created a dependency map identifying vendor touchpoints and timelines
- Introduced early dependency visibility during backlog refinement
- Worked with Product Owner to slice stories to reduce dependency risk
- Facilitated cross-team syncs to proactively track integration readiness
- Adjusted sprint planning to prioritize dependency-free work where possible

## Outcome
- Reduced dependency-related blockers during sprint execution
- Improved sprint predictability by approximately 30%
- Maintained team productivity despite vendor delays
- Increased stakeholder confidence through transparency

## Key Learning
Early visibility and proactive dependency management significantly reduce delivery risk in integration-heavy programs.
