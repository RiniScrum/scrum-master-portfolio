# Stakeholder Conflict Resolution

## Scenario
Resolving conflicting stakeholder priorities during sprint execution.

## Context
Enterprise delivery program involving multiple business stakeholders and Scrum teams delivering customer-facing functionality.

## Challenge
Different stakeholders pushed competing priorities, creating pressure to add work mid-sprint and risking sprint goals.

## Action Taken
- Facilitated a structured priority discussion with stakeholders
- Used sprint goals, velocity data, and capacity insights to guide decisions
- Presented trade-off options transparently
- Reinforced Scrum principles around sprint commitment
- Ensured respectful, data-driven communication

## Outcome
- Reduced mid-sprint scope change requests by **40%**
- Improved sprint goal success rate to **90%+**
- Reduced stakeholder escalations by **30%**
- Strengthened trust and alignment across teams

## Key Learning
Data-driven facilitation and clear Scrum boundaries help resolve conflicts while maintaining strong stakeholder relationships.
