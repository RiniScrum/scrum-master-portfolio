# Regulatory Change Handling

## Scenario
Adapting to regulatory changes during active delivery cycles.

## Context
Patient Health Management System operating in a regulated healthcare environment with strict compliance and audit requirements.

## Challenge
A regulatory update was introduced mid-release, impacting scope, documentation, and delivery timelines.

## Action Taken
- Partnered with Product Owner to assess regulatory impact
- Facilitated backlog re-prioritization to incorporate mandatory changes
- Ensured traceability and compliance requirements were addressed
- Maintained Agile cadence while meeting audit expectations
- Communicated delivery impact clearly to stakeholders

## Outcome
- Reduced late-stage compliance rework by **30–35%**
- Improved release predictability despite regulatory changes
- Achieved **100% audit readiness** for regulatory checkpoints
- Increased stakeholder confidence in Agile delivery

## Key Learning
Agile delivery and regulatory compliance can coexist when governance is integrated thoughtfully into Scrum practices.
