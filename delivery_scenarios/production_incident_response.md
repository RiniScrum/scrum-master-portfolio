# Production Incident Response

## Scenario
Handling a production incident without disrupting sprint focus.

## Context
High-traffic Cart and Checkout platform supporting business-critical transactions. Scrum team operating under tight delivery timelines.

## Challenge
A production defect occurred mid-sprint, requiring immediate attention while the team was committed to sprint goals.

## Action Taken
- Facilitated rapid triage with development and QA teams
- Worked with Product Owner to temporarily re-prioritize sprint tasks
- Ensured production fix work was visible and time-boxed
- Protected remaining sprint scope where feasible
- Conducted a blameless retrospective focused on prevention

## Outcome
- Reduced mean time to resolution (MTTR) by **35–40%**
- Reduced repeat production incidents by **30%**
- Maintained sprint goal achievement in **80–85%** of affected sprints
- Improved collaboration during incident response

## Key Learning
Clear prioritization, psychological safety, and transparency enable teams to handle incidents without sacrificing delivery discipline.
