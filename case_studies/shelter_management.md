📌 Project 4: Shelter Management System (Rescue Cats & Dogs)

Case Study Title

Scaling Agile Delivery for a Mission-Driven Platform

Context
	•	Domain: Non-profit / Animal Welfare
	•	Teams: 1–2 small Scrum teams
	•	Stakeholders: NGOs, volunteers, operations teams

Challenges
	•	Limited resources
	•	Changing stakeholder priorities
	•	Small team bandwidth

Scrum Master Actions
	•	Simplified Scrum framework for small teams
	•	Focused on MVP and incremental delivery
	•	Balanced stakeholder needs with team capacity
	•	Enabled fast feedback loops

Metrics Used
	•	Sprint goal success rate
	•	Cycle time
	•	Stakeholder satisfaction (qualitative)

Outcomes
	•	Faster delivery of usable features
	•	Improved alignment with real-world needs
	•	High team engagement despite constraints

Real Delivery Scenario

Conflicting stakeholder requests
→ Facilitated priority workshop
→ Achieved alignment without overloading team
