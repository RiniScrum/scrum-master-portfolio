# 📌 Project 3: Patient Health Management System

## Case Study Title
**Delivering Agile in a Regulated Healthcare Environment**

---

## Context
- **Domain:** Healthcare / Patient Systems  
- **Teams:** 4 cross-functional Scrum teams  
- **Constraints:** Regulatory compliance, audits, data security  

---

## Project Overview

Led Agile delivery of a Patient Health Management System in a highly regulated healthcare environment, ensuring compliance, audit readiness, 
and secure handling of patient data while maintaining predictable delivery.

---

## Role

Scrum Master & Technical Project Manager

---

## Challenges
- Heavy documentation and audit requirements  
- Frequent regulatory changes impacting scope and timelines  
- Initial Agile skepticism from business and compliance stakeholders
- Maintaining velocity while ensuring audit readiness

---

## Key Responsibilities

- Led 4 cross-functional Scrum teams delivering patient-facing and internal healthcare system capabilities.
- Facilitated Scrum ceremonies tailored to include compliance, audit, and security checkpoints without impacting delivery velocity.
- Partnered closely with Product Owners, compliance, and security teams to balance Agile delivery with regulatory documentation requirements.
- Oversaw end-to-end technical delivery, including system integrations, data security considerations, release planning, and deployment readiness.
- Ensured end-to-end traceability across requirements, design, implementation, and releases to support audits and regulatory reviews.
- Guided teams through mid-release regulatory changes by facilitating backlog reprioritization and transparent stakeholder communication.

--

## Metrics & Outcomes
- Delivered predictable, audit-ready releases in a regulated environment
- Reduced late-stage compliance rework and release delays
- Improved stakeholder trust in Agile delivery and governance
- Maintained delivery predictability despite regulatory changes  

---

## Tool & Tech Stack
Java, Spring Boot, Microservices, SQL, Docker, Jenkins, AWS,
Jira, Confluence, Agile/Scrum 

---

## Real Delivery Scenario

### Regulatory Change Introduced Mid-Release
- Facilitated backlog re-prioritization to incorporate mandatory changes  
- Maintained delivery transparency and stakeholder alignment throughout the release
