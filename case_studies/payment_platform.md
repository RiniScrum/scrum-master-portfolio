# 📌 Project 1: Payment Platform (Gift Card, Credit Card, PayPal, Apple Pay)

## Case Study Title
**Enabling Predictable Delivery for Multi-Channel Payment Integrations**

---

## Context
- **Domain:** E-commerce / Payments  
- **Teams:** 3 Scrum teams (Backend, Integration, QA)  
- **Environment:** Java, Spring Boot, Microservices, AWS  
- **Delivery Model:** Scrum with external dependencies  

---

## Challenges
- Multiple payment providers with different APIs  
- Security, compliance, and certification timelines  
- High dependency on external vendors  
- Frequent mid-sprint change requests  

---

## Scrum Master Actions
- Facilitated **refined backlog slicing** by payment method and integration readiness  
- Introduced **dependency mapping** for external payment integrations  
- Enforced **Definition of Ready** for payment-related user stories  
- Protected sprint scope while educating stakeholders on Scrum principles  
- Enabled regular **cross-team syncs** to track integration readiness  

---

## Metrics Used
- Sprint commitment reliability  
- Cycle time for payment integration stories  
- Post-release defect leakage  

---

## Outcomes
- Improved sprint predictability by **30%**  
- Reduced payment-related production defects  
- Achieved more predictable release windows despite vendor dependencies  

---

## Real Delivery Scenario

### Urgent Apple Pay Change Request Mid-Sprint
- Used velocity and capacity data to explain sprint constraints  
- Collaborated with Product Owner to reprioritize the request for the next sprint  
- Maintained sprint focus while preserving stakeholder trust
